Mathematics

Course Notes

1. Arithmetic and Units

Follow the order of operations: brackets, powers and roots, multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction.

For fractions, multiply numerators and denominators to multiply; to divide, multiply by the reciprocal. Convert a percentage to a decimal by dividing by 100. For example, 15% = 0.15.

Percentage change = (change ÷ original value) × 100%

Use scientific notation for very large or small values: 4,500 = 4.5 × 10³ and 0.0062 = 6.2 × 10⁻³. Convert quantities to compatible units before calculating.

Useful conversions: 1 km = 1,000 m; 1 hour = 3,600 seconds; 1 tonne = 1,000 kg. A knot is one nautical mile per hour.

Speed = distance ÷ time

2. Algebra

Letters represent unknown or changing values. To solve an equation, perform the same operation on both sides.

3x + 5 = 20 → 3x = 15 → x = 5

When rearranging a formula, undo operations in reverse order. For example, from v = d ÷ t, multiply by t and then divide by v to get t = d ÷ v. Keep units consistent when substituting values.

3. Geometry

Perimeter is the distance around a shape; area measures a flat surface; volume measures three-dimensional space.

Rectangle: A = length × width
Triangle: A = ½ × base × height
Circle: circumference = 2πr; area = πr²
Rectangular prism: V = length × width × height
Cylinder: V = πr²h

Area uses square units, such as cm². Volume uses cubic units, such as cm³. In a right-angled triangle, the hypotenuse is opposite the right angle:

a² + b² = c²

4. Trigonometry

For a right-angled triangle and angle θ, identify the opposite side, adjacent side and hypotenuse relative to that angle.

sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse
cos θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse
tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent

A useful reminder is SOH-CAH-TOA. Check whether your calculator is set to degrees or radians before calculating.

5. Vectors

A vector has magnitude and direction, such as force or velocity. A scalar has magnitude only, such as mass.

For perpendicular vectors, calculate the resultant magnitude using Pythagoras. For a vector V at angle θ measured from the horizontal:

Horizontal component: Vx = V cos θ
Vertical component: Vy = V sin θ

Choose and keep consistent coordinate directions. Components pointing opposite to a positive axis are negative.
